# Patent pledge

Nu-Time Inc. wants the Nu:Time Standard to be implementable by anyone, anywhere, without royalties or permission.

**Pledge.** Nu-Time Inc., on behalf of itself and its affiliates, irrevocably promises not to assert any patent claim it owns or controls, now or in the future, against any person or organization for making, using, selling, offering for sale, importing or distributing any implementation of the Nu:Time Standard, to the extent that the claim is necessarily infringed by implementing the Standard.

**Defensive termination.** This pledge does not apply to anyone who asserts a patent claim against Nu-Time Inc., its affiliates, or any implementer of the Nu:Time Standard, alleging that an implementation of the Standard infringes.

**Scope.** The pledge covers the Standard as published by Nu-Time Inc. (all versions) and the reference implementations in this repository. It does not cover product designs that go beyond the Standard, such as a particular watch case or industrial design.

Nu-Time Inc. intends additionally to make the Standard available under the Open Web Foundation Final Specification Agreement (OWFa 1.0) when v1.0 is final.
