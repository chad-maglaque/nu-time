# Privacy

**Controller:** Nu-Time Inc. · **Contact:** privacy@nu-time.org · **Updated:** 2026-11-19

When you join the Nu:Time list we collect what you type into the form (email, and optionally name, role, country and message), plus your browser language and time zone, the page you signed up from, and the country our host derives from your connection. We email you Nu:Time news only if you tick the box on the form; otherwise we use it only to understand who is interested in the standard and to reply to your message. We keep it until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it. We do not sell it or share it for advertising.

The data is stored with our hosting providers (Vercel, and Upstash for the database), which may process it in the United States or other countries. By submitting the form you consent to that transfer, including, if you are in mainland China, the transfer of your personal information outside China.

The clock asks your browser for your location only if you choose "Current Location (Auto-Detect)" or "Use my location". If the browser can't or won't share it, the clock asks our host for the approximate location (usually the nearest city) that it derives from your connection. Either way the location is used only in your browser to place the sun; it is not stored or sent to us.

You can unsubscribe at any time from any email, and ask us to see, correct or delete your data by writing to privacy@nu-time.org. These rights apply to everyone, wherever you live, and they meet the GDPR, the UK GDPR and China's PIPL. You can also complain to the data-protection authority in your country.
