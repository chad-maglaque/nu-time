# Privacy

**Controller:** Nu-Time Inc. · **Contact:** privacy@nu-time.com · **Updated:** 2026-11-17

When you join the Nu:Time list we collect what you type into the form (email, and optionally name, role, country and message), plus your browser language and time zone, the page you signed up from, and the country our host derives from your connection. We use it only to send you Nu:Time news and to understand who is interested in the standard. We do not sell it or share it for advertising.

The data is stored with our hosting providers (Vercel, and Upstash for the database), which may process it in the United States or other countries. By submitting the form you consent to that transfer, including, if you are in mainland China, the transfer of your personal information outside China.

The clock asks your browser for your location only if you choose "Current Location". If the browser can't or won't share it, the clock asks our host for the approximate location (usually the nearest city) that it derives from your connection. Either way the location is used only in your browser to place the sun; it is not stored or sent to us.

You can unsubscribe at any time from any email, and ask us to see, correct or delete your data by writing to privacy@nu-time.com. EU, UK and Chinese residents have additional rights under the GDPR, UK GDPR and PIPL, which we honour.
