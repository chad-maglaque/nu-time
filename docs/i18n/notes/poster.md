# Share poster strings: translation, review and back-translation

Added 2026-11-19 with the share poster on the home page (`hero.tryIt` is the button; `poster.*` are the dialog and the image text; `poster.button` is now only the image's alt text). Each language was translated, then independently reviewed and back-translated into literal English; the review's fixes were applied. The Urdu poster was checked rendered. Unit words follow `units.ke` in each locale (刻, Кэ, Kè).

## zh

| Key | English source | Translation | Back-translation |
|---|---|---|---|
| `tryIt` | Try it for yourself! | 亲手试试！ | Try it with your own hands! |
| `hint` | Long-press the image to save it, or use the buttons below. | 长按图片即可保存，也可以用下方按钮保存或分享到朋友圈、小红书。 | Long-press the image to save it; you can also use the buttons below to save it or share it to WeChat Moments or Xiaohongshu. |
| `save` | Save image | 保存图片 | Save image |
| `share` | Share | 分享 | Share |
| `close` | Close | 关闭 | Close |
| `tagline` | 1 Kè = 864 seconds · 100 Kè in a day, 100 Kè in a circle | 一刻 = 864 秒 · 一天 100 刻，一圈也是 100 刻 | One kè = 864 s · 100 kè in a day, a circle is also 100 kè |
| `scan` | Scan for the live dial | 扫码看实时表盘 | Scan to see the real-time dial |
| `place` | My location | 我的位置 | My location |
| `button` | Make my poster | 生成我的刻时海报 | Generate my Kèshí poster |

Review changes: hint: now mentions the buttons (desktop can't long-press) and ends with 。
