# pt — translator notes

- **Units: gong / kè / beat / tique** (plurals gongs / kè / beats / tiques), all masculine: o gong, o kè, o beat, o tique. **Tique** rather than *tick*: it is a real Portuguese word, it carries the clock sound (*tique-taque*), it takes a normal plural and it matches the tic of fr/it/es. Its other meaning (a nervous tic) never comes up in context. **Beat** stays in English per the glossary; Brazilian readers know it from music (*beat*, *beatmaker*), and *batida* would collide with "heartbeat" in the tick card. **Kè** is invariable in the plural (100 kè), as in English. Prose pattern: gong:beat:tique.
- **Register:** você throughout, plain and direct, no *tu* and no *a gente* (which reads too casual in Portugal). Brazilian spelling (astrônomos, atômicos, Babilônia, detecção, contato, sinônimo). It stays readable in Portugal, but a Portuguese reader will notice the Brazilian forms. That is intended.
- **Numbers:** decimal comma and dot thousands everywhere, including the tables and `n` fields: 1.000, 100.000, 86.400, 16,67 kè, 12,5 kè, 3,456 MHz, 32,768 kHz, 28.311,552, 2.985.984. Digits unchanged.
- **"Standard" = padrão**, capitalised as a name for the document ("o Padrão Nu:Time", nav "O Padrão"). *Padrão aberto* is the usual Brazilian term for an open standard; *norma* sounds like ABNT/ISO paperwork. "Standards bodies" = *organismos de normalização*, which is the technical term.
- **Leap second = segundo bissexto**, the term Brazilian media and the pt Wikipedia use. *Segundo intercalar* is more technical. "Daylight saving" = *horário de verão* (Brazil abolished it in 2019, so it lands well, but I added nothing). "Time zone" = *fuso horário*. "International Date Line" = *Linha Internacional de Data*.
- **"A billion" = um bilhão** (10⁹ in Brazil). In Portugal *bilião* means 10¹², but *um bilhão* is widely recognised there as the Brazilian form.
- **Trig notation: sen / cos / tg**, as taught in Brazilian (and Portuguese) schools. Revert to sin/tan if the table must match calculator keys.
- **Compass letters:** E is **L** (leste) and W is **O** (oeste), as Brazilian maps do. Portugal would use E (este).
- **Gender:** roles are field names under the label "Sua área" (Desenvolvimento de software, Criação de hardware, Pesquisa / academia, Normalização / governo, Criação de conteúdo, Investimento / parcerias, Pura curiosidade). `join.lead` uses verbs ("Se você desenvolve software, cria hardware…") instead of masculine plurals, and `join.title` is "Saia na frente" instead of *Seja um dos primeiros*. "Welcome" = *Boas-vindas* (neutral). Nav "Developers" = *Desenvolvimento*, link "Developer docs" = *Documentação técnica*.
- **Kept in English:** "Nu:Time Certified" (mark name), "National Time Service Center" (DECISIONS #15), and *royalties* (standard in Brazilian contracts). I avoided *maker*: "Hardware maker" became *Criação de hardware*. The quoted claim "Nu:Time compatible" became "compatível com Nu:Time", as es/it/fr did. Revert if the claim must stay in English.
- **English that didn't carry over:** "It's a finished one" became *É uma ideia pronta* (*acabada* also means "worn out" in Brazil). `time.lead` repeats "it doesn't change the time", so I dropped the repetition. "It's time" became *Chegou a hora*, and "That's how we know the time is now" became *É por isso que sabemos que chegou a hora*. "Where the sun is overhead" (`time.points[3]`) became *a posição do sol no céu*, and "overhead" in `points[2]` became *a pino*. "Kè" capitalised mid-sentence in `how.cards[3]` is lowercased.
- **`why.items[2]`:** "separates the shared time from the local sun" became *separa duas coisas: a hora que todos compartilham e o sol de cada lugar*. A literal *separa a hora compartilhada do sol* reads as "the time shared by the sun".
- **Slogan:** "time and location reimagined" became *tempo e lugar, reinventados*. It is shorter and more natural than *tempo e localização*. *Localização* is used for the UI (the user's position).
- **Cities**, using Brazilian media forms: Pequim, Berlim, Bogotá, Cidade do Cabo, Déli, Helsinque, Istambul, Jacarta, Joanesburgo, Londres, Madri, Cidade do México, Moscou, Nairóbi, Nova York, Riad, Roma, São Francisco, São Paulo, Seul, Xangai, Singapura, Estocolmo, Taipé, Tóquio. Kept: Bangkok, Karachi, Reykjavik, Sydney, Mumbai. Uncertain: **Déli** vs *Délhi* (G1/Folha write *Nova Déli*, Estadão *Nova Délhi*) and **São Francisco** vs *San Francisco* (both are common). Portugal would say Nova Iorque, Moscovo, Banguecoque.
- **Privacy:** "Controller" = *Controlador* (the LGPD term, but the LGPD itself is not mentioned). The laws are named exactly as in the English: GDPR, UK GDPR, PIPL. The location buttons are quoted with the exact UI labels.
