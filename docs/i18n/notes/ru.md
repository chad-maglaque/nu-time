# Russian (ru) — translator notes

- **Register: «вы», lowercase.** Russian tech products and launch pages that talk to engineers, press and standards people (Яндекс, JetBrains, most SaaS) use «вы». From an unknown brand, «ты» reads as overfamiliar, especially to the standards, government and press readers in the form. Lowercase «вы» is the web norm; capital «Вы» belongs in personal letters and official correspondence and would feel stiff. Headings use the imperative or no address at all («Будьте первыми», «Попробуйте сами!»), so the tone stays confident, not formal.
- **гонг** — masculine, declines normally: гонг, 2 гонга, 10 гонгов. Plain Cyrillic loan of the brand unit; the word already exists in Russian.
- **кэ** — the standard Palladius transcription of Chinese 刻 *kè*, so sinologists will recognise it. Like other foreign nouns ending in a vowel (кафе, кенгуру) it is **indeclinable and neuter**: «одно кэ», «100 кэ», «то самое кэ», «каждое кэ». Capitalised only at the start of a heading or label (Кэ). For the same reason the night watch 更 is **гэн (更)** (Palladius for *gēng*), also in the licence line «гонг (гэн 更)».
- **удар** (decided in review, replacing the draft's «бит») — удар, 2 удара, 1 000 ударов. "Beat" is the everyday word, as in heartbeat, and the everyday Russian for that is «удар» (удар сердца, 60 ударов в минуту, удары часов). It pairs naturally with the struck гонг and has no technical clash. «бит» was dropped because, for the engineers this page addresses, it is first of all the information *bit* (and «битов» is used for data bits too, so declension does not separate them). «такт» was rejected because it is the CPU/quartz clock cycle, exactly the sense of the quartz table. To keep «удар» unambiguous, the tick card says «Близко к ритму сердца в покое» rather than «один удар сердца».
- **тик** — тик, 2 тика, 100 000 тиков. Matches «тиканье» of a clock; no real clash.
- **Units after numbers** use the counting form (10 гонгов · 1 000 ударов · 100 000 тиков · 100 кэ), so `how.cards[].unit` is genitive plural to agree with the big number above it. `units.gongs` (the day-bar axis label) is nominative plural «гонги». The prose pattern is **гонг:удар:тик**.
- **«universal Nu:Time» → «единое время Nu:Time».** «Всемирное время» is the established Russian name of UT, so it would read as UTC/UT, the same trap fr and de noted. «Универсальное» sounds translated.
- **Typography**: decimal comma (16,67 кэ; 3,456 МГц), narrow no-break space U+202F for thousands (1 000, 2 985 984, 28 311,552), U+00A0 between number and unit and before every spaced em dash. «Ёлочки», ё throughout. MHz/kHz → МГц/кГц. Year ranges without spaces (1793–1795, 2030–2035), as Russian typesetting requires; «Древность — XVII век» with a Roman-numeral century.
- **Trig notation: tg**, not tan, in the table header. It is what every Russian school and university textbook uses; sin/cos unchanged. Revert to tan if the column must match calculator keys.
- **Roles** are fields, not people (Разработка ПО, Создание устройств, Наука / исследования, Стандартизация / госсектор, Контент / блогинг…), under «Чем вы занимаетесь?». `join.lead` uses gender-neutral present-tense verbs («Пишете код или паяете…») instead of a list of masculine nouns. `nav.build` is «Разработчикам», the usual Russian nav label; the masculine plural there is generic, not a role label.
- **Rebuilt rather than translated**: «время и место — по-новому» (hero/tagline; «по-новому» echoes *Nu*); «Nu:Time отменяет.» + accusative list (the heading → lead-in structure works in Russian); «Ваше место под солнцем» (a neutral Russian idiom, unlike German); «Пора.» for «It's time.»; «Поэтому мы и знаем: время пришло.»; «десятичное время просто опередило своё время» for "was just too early"; «Помогите задать стандарт» keeps the "set the standard" double meaning. `time.lead`'s repeated "it doesn't change the time" said once.
- **"Temporal data"** (roadmap 2030–2035): rephrased in review as «данных о времени и геоданных», which avoids the "temporary data" reading without a stress mark.
- **"National Time Service Center"**: translated in review as «Национальный центр службы времени Китайской академии наук», the form used in Russian tech press. BeiDou → «Бэйдоу» (standard Russian form). «Nu:Time Certified» kept as a mark; "Nu:Time compatible" localised as «совместимые с Nu:Time» (descriptive claim; legal to confirm).
- **Privacy page**: "Controller" → «Ответственный за обработку данных», deliberately not «оператор» (the term of the Russian data law 152-ФЗ, which the English does not reference) or «контролёр» (sounds like a ticket inspector). Laws kept exactly as GDPR, UK GDPR, PIPL. "join the list" → «подписываетесь на рассылку»; "role" → «род занятий» to match the form.
- **Gender of Nu:Time**: sentences are built to avoid past tense or adjectives agreeing with the brand («Nu:Time следует…», «ему не понадобится»). 刻时 Ke-Shi is treated as masculine (стандарт): «уходящий корнями».
- **Cities**: standard Russian forms: Пекин, Мумбаи, Нью-Йорк, Мехико, Эр-Рияд, Тайбэй, Сан-Паулу, Кейптаун, Гонконг, Куала-Лумпур, Ушуая, Рейкьявик. «Washington D.C.» → «Вашингтон» (the clock appends the country, so it is unambiguous); «Santiago» → «Сантьяго».
