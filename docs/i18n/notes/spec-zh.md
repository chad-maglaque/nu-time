# Standard (/spec/) zh (简体中文): translator notes

- **Register.** This is a standards document, so the voice is 书面语: 一日 rather than 一天, 规定, 实现, and 本标准/本文件. Terms are taken from the site wherever it already has them: 统一刻时, 本初子午线（格林尼治）, 全球统一同步, 周角（转一整圈）, 平角 · 三角形内角和, 刻值/度数, 周期数 / 刻时秒, 分频方式, 精确整数分频, and 兼容刻时 Nu:Time.
- **Normative words.** These follow GB/T 1.1: must/shall → **应**, must not/must never → **不应**, should → **宜**, may → **可**. Bold appears only where the English has bold. The unbolded "never" clauses (1.1 "never round ahead" and §4 "never a decimal value…") are also rendered as 不应, so 不得 is never used. Lowercase descriptive "may" (as in "may be used alongside" or "the sun may not rise") is rendered as 可 or 可能, without bold. The §6 conformance sentence stays descriptive, as in the English.
- **Brand line.** The English reads "Nu:Time™ (刻时 Ke-Shi™)". To follow the brief's "lead with 刻时", it becomes **刻时 Nu:Time™（Ke-Shi™）**, which keeps both ™ marks. In §8 the trademark list is byte-identical (Nu:Time™, Ke-Shi™, 刻时™).
- **刻时分 / 刻时秒.** The bare forms 秒 and 分 are used in unit tables, in readings, and in the GG:BB:TT description. Wherever a sentence also involves SI time (0.864 s, UTC, leap seconds, the crystal and ppm figures), the forms are 刻时秒 and 刻时分, as §1.2 itself prescribes. Example: 1 个国际单位制秒 = 1.157407… 刻时秒.
- **Unit table (§1).** The column "Unit" became **英文名** and keeps the English names (Day, **Gong**…). Translating them would only duplicate the 中文名 column. Subdivision cells use Chinese units (10 刻 · 100 分). Durations keep the SI symbols s / h / min, which is GB practice. The site has no localised duration units.
- **Numbers.** Table cells are byte-identical in their digits and separators. Prose follows the site: 86400 and 1000 have no separator, and 100,000 ticks is written **10 万秒**, as in the site's hero. Please check whether 100000 秒 is preferred inside the equation chain in §1.
- **"gong 5, kè 53, beat 537 of the day" (§1.1).** This is rendered as 按当日计为 5 更、53 刻、537 分. I avoided 第 5 更 because it suggests a 1-based count, and 五更 because that is the reading of the whole value. A reviewer should confirm the reading.
- **"Gong `00:00:00`" (§2).** This is rendered as 0 更 `00:00:00`. The English is slightly odd, since it pairs a unit with a full readout.
- **Nu:Day number** → **Nu:Day 日序数**, glossed as 简化儒略日数. I did not add "MJD", because the English doesn't use it. An alternative is Nu:Day 编号.
- **Reference names (§2.3).** The identifiers stay as code, followed by a gloss: `utc`（统一）, `solar`（平太阳时）, `ke`（按刻取整的太阳时）, and `civil`（沿用的民用时）. "Legacy" is rendered as 沿用的 rather than 传统, which sounds cultural, or 旧, which sounds pejorative. "Wall-clock" becomes 民用钟表时间.
- **Astronomy and hardware terms:** 平太阳日, 视太阳正午（真太阳正午）, 时差 (equation of time), 地平俯角 (horizon depression), and 国际地球自转和参考系服务（IERS） (common mainland form; 与 is also seen). "Discipline via GNSS/NTP" is rendered as **驯服**, the established engineering term (GNSS 驯服时钟). The 3.456 MHz row reads 刻时参考晶振, while the site's quartz table says 首选晶振. The English says "reference", so the standard uses 参考. Consider aligning the site.
- **Coordinates.** Nu:Long and Nu:Lat use 向东/向西 and 向北/向南 with sign, matching the site's 本初子午线以东或以西. The city examples (§4) write 东经 121.47° = 向东 33.74 刻 instead of the Latin "E/W" suffixes.
- **§8 legal lines.** "Nu:Time compatible" is rendered as “兼容刻时 Nu:Time” and "Nu:Time Certified" as **刻时 Nu:Time 认证**, both matching the site. Legal should confirm that the claim and mark wording may be localised, rather than kept as the English strings. The patent pledge has been restructured for Chinese syntax without changing its scope.
- **§9 "network time protocol profile"** → 网络时间协议的应用规范（profile）. "Profile" has no settled Chinese term, so the English was kept as a gloss.
- **Not changed.** All 3 code blocks, the JSON, every inline `code` span, the test vectors, §5, CC BY 4.0, Nu-Time Inc. and the dates are byte-identical. The comment inside the solarNoon code block, `(X negative for west)`, is left in English to keep the block identical; the prose after it already covers the sign convention. Nothing on politics, borders or history was added.
