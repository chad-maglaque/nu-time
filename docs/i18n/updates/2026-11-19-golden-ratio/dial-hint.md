Current: Nu:Timeは、どこにいても、誰にとっても同じです。ただし、普通の時計とは違い、Nu:Timeの文字盤にある4つの座標目盛りは、グリニッジの本初子午線を固定の基準にしたまま、あなたのいる場所に合わせて回転します。
Back-translation: "Nu:Time is the same for everyone, wherever you are. Provided, however, that unlike an ordinary clock, the four coordinate marks on the Nu:Time dial rotate to match your location while keeping Greenwich's Prime Meridian as a fixed reference."
Notes: ただし is a proviso or exception marker, the kind used in fine print and terms ("ただし、〜を除く"). Here it reads as if it limits the first sentence, as though the time is the same "except that…", which undercuts the claim. The English "But" is a plain contrast. でも、 matches it in strength and suits the warm です・ます voice of the page. けれども、 or ですが、 would also work if the owner wants something a little more formal. とはいえ ("that said") is acceptable but adds a slight concessive weight that the English lacks. The comma after the conjunction is correct. ただし appears nowhere else in ja.json at sentence start, so no other string uses it.
Corrected: Nu:Timeは、どこにいても、誰にとっても同じです。でも、普通の時計とは違い、Nu:Timeの文字盤にある4つの座標目盛りは、グリニッジの本初子午線を固定の基準にしたまま、あなたのいる場所に合わせて回転します。
