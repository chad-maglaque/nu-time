# hero.sub review: "space" -> "location"

English (final): "One clock for the whole planet connecting time and location. Measuring both when and where you are on Earth."

Only the time+location phrase changed. The second sentence in every locale is unchanged and was already faithful.

## zh
- Title term: 位置 (时间与位置，重新想象。)
- Current: 整个地球，共用一座钟，把时间和位置连在一起。既能度量你身处何时，也能度量你在地球上的何处。
- Back-translation: "The whole Earth shares one clock, which links time and location together. It can measure both when you are and where on Earth you are."
- Verdict: **pass**. 把时间和位置连在一起 reads naturally; matches the heading.

## ja

- Back-translation: "One clock for the whole Earth, connecting time and place. It measures both the 'when' and the 'where' on Earth."
- Verdict: **pass**. 時間と場所 is a natural pair; matches the heading.

## ur (unchanged)
- Title term: مقام (وقت اور مقام کا نیا تصور۔)
- Current: پوری دنیا کے لیے ایک ہی گھڑی، جو وقت اور جگہ کو جوڑتی ہے۔ یہ ناپتی ہے کہ زمین پر آپ کب ہیں — اور کہاں بھی۔
- Back-translation: "One single clock for the whole world, which connects time and place. It measures when you are on Earth — and where, too."
- Verdict: **fix (consistency only, low priority)**. Meaning is correct and جگہ is natural, but the heading uses مقام, not جگہ. Every other locale now uses the heading's word, so Urdu is the one exception. Swapping is grammatically safe: جوڑتی ہے agrees with گھڑی (feminine), not with the object, so nothing else changes.
- Corrected: پوری دنیا کے لیے ایک ہی گھڑی، جو وقت اور مقام کو جوڑتی ہے۔ یہ ناپتی ہے کہ زمین پر آپ کب ہیں — اور کہاں بھی۔
- If the owner prefers the more colloquial جگہ, leaving it as-is is also acceptable.

- Back-translation: "A single clock for the whole planet, which unites time and place. It measures at once when and where you are on Earth."

## de
- Title term: Ort (Zeit und Ort neu gedacht.)
- Current: Eine Uhr für den ganzen Planeten, die Zeit und Ort verbindet. Sie misst zugleich, wann und wo auf der Erde du gerade bist.
- Back-translation: "A clock for the whole planet that connects time and place. It measures at the same time when and where on Earth you are right now."
- Verdict: **pass**. There is no article-agreement problem: "die" is the relative pronoun for "Uhr" (feminine), not an article on "Zeit". "Zeit und Ort" are bare nouns, the same as the heading and as "Zeit und Raum" before.
